Forking this skill targeting neurodivergent individuals with avoidance coping mechanisms to help format my own AI chat responses.
The fork is mainly motivated by the absolute need I have to rename it "ClAuDHD" as the creator intended, but without the visual clutter, making it much more typeable, readable and aesthetically pleasing for my ADHD brain that is both prone to distraction (aka: showing significant avoidance behaviours) and has enough autistic traits to have been asked if they wanted it written on their assessment: visual stimulation is not my thing and therefore, the alternance of capital letters and lower case letters in the name originally imagined by the original skill writer was, in my honest and personal opinion, the key to the risk of poor understanding of the aforementioned punny name. 

I intend to keep it synced with upstream. I might create another branch in the future for my personal needs and maintain both in the spirit of realising the initial naming wish of the original developer. It'll also be excellent git usage practice. It's cherry picking season, my friends!
