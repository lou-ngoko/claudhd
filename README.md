Forking this skill targeting neurodivergent individuals with avoidance coping mechanisms to help format my own AI chat responses.
The fork is mainly motivated by the absolute need I have to rename it "ClAuDHD" as the creator intended, but without the visual clutter, making it much more typeable, readable and aesthetically pleasing for my ADHD brain. I am both prone to distraction (as well as showing significant avoidance behaviours) and have enough autistic traits to have been asked if I wanted it written on my assessment: visual stimulation is not my thing and therefore, the alternance of capital letters and lower case letters in the name originally imagined by the original skill writer was, in my honest and personal opinion, the key to the risk of poor understanding of the aforementioned punny name. 

I intend to keep it synced with upstream. I might create another branch in the future for my personal adult ADHD with avoidance behaviours needs and maintain both in the spirit of realising the initial naming wish of the original developer. It'll also be excellent git usage practice. It's cherry picking season, my friends!
